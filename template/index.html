Questions & safety

Know what the assistant can and cannot do

LegalEase provides preliminary educational guidance. It does not replace a lawyer, court, police authority, emergency service or official legal-aid provider.

Ask Your Question
Is LegalEase a replacement for a lawyer?

No. It can help organise an issue, explain general possibilities and suggest questions or next steps. A qualified professional should handle personalised legal advice, representation and high-risk decisions.

What information should I avoid entering?

Do not share passwords, OTPs, complete payment-card details, account credentials or unnecessary identity numbers. Describe the incident using only the information needed to understand it.

Can I use voice or attach a document?

Yes. The LegalEase assistant supports voice input and common document formats. Review a document before uploading it and remove information that is not necessary for the question.

Does the assistant support Indian languages?

The interface is designed to support English, Hindi, Hinglish and several regional languages, subject to browser and AI-model capabilities.

What should I do in an emergency?

Do not wait for an AI response. Contact the appropriate emergency service, police authority, medical provider, trusted person or qualified legal professional immediately.
